# Safety and interpretation

**Research only: these images/videos are not therapy, diagnosis, or an evidence-backed eye exercise.**

- A flat screen remains at a fixed physical focal distance. Software blur cues cannot physically move its optical focal plane.
- Perceived depth, perceived blur and discomfort are not measurements of the ciliary muscle or accommodation.
- Do not force focus or prolong uncomfortable viewing. Stop immediately if symptoms such as headache, pain, nausea or double vision occur.
- Persistent or worsening symptoms merit assessment by a qualified eye-care professional.
- Proper breaks, blinking, screen ergonomics and appropriate vision correction are important regardless of these experiments.
- Human tests require calibrated objective instrumentation, informed consent, supervision and suitable ethics review.
