# GazeBloom roadmap

Two separate tracks: (A) optical/physiological research and (B) a possible future screen-break application.

## Research
- [x] Gaussian/LCA example image and short-video generation.
- [x] Wave-optics PSF simulation and model-internal sanity tests.
- [x] Synthetic checks for a future objective accommodation measurement pipeline.
- [ ] Independent Q1 optical validation and robust retinal-image optimization.
- [ ] Parameter sensitivity and monitor color calibration.
- [ ] Pre-registered supervised Q2 study measuring accommodation objectively.
- [ ] Controlled Q3 study measuring comfort compared with equivalent rest breaks.

## Potential wellness app (not implemented yet)
- [ ] Accessible reminder to take screen breaks and look into the distance.
- [ ] User-controlled prompts, a stop control and comfort settings.
- [ ] Experimental visual stimuli only if evidence supports safety/utility.
- [ ] Privacy-first app design and careful claims supported by evidence.

No validated exercise or digital-eye-strain treatment currently exists in this repository.
